The Live Activity, everywhere it appears

Drawn at Apple's sizes in points, with option E on the Lock Screen and the other places made to match it. Checked against Apple's guidelines (13 October 2026). Sample family only.

What Apple asks, and where we are

✓Lock Screen margin of 14 points, a custom dark background, the dismiss button coloured to match.
✓One Live Activity for several things: one card for the whole family, not one per child.
✓Ends at once when there is nothing to show; no alerts or sounds; tapping opens the right sheet.
✓The Smart Stack and CarPlay have their own small layout, with no buttons (CarPlay switches them off).
✓Key line in the child's colour; height changes with what there is to show.
!Limited width is new and missing. In landscape the island gives each side a 28-point circle. Apple asks for a short form there, or the leading side alone. We draw neither, so text can be cut off.
!Minimal should show live information, not only a logo. Ours shows the first child's initial. Apple's own Timer shows the time left.
!The expanded island should look like the Lock Screen. Ours still has one big number and a line of small text; the Lock Screen now has three columns.
!Text a medium weight or heavier. Some small words are regular weight (the line under the name in the expanded island).
!Always-On, dimmed: the paler words (70% cream) need checking on a phone with Always-On; Apple asks to check contrast there.
!Sensitive information. Apple suggests a choice to hide details on the Lock Screen. A child's name and their feeds show to anyone who picks up the phone. Suggested: a setting to show initials only.
!Activities with a beginning and end, under eight hours. A sleep or a feed is one. The card between them (the "since" card) runs all day, which is why iOS asked "continue to allow Live Activities from Puggle?" in the simulator today. Worth keeping, but it needs to end and restart cleanly at the eight-hour limit, and the setting to turn it off stays easy to find.
!One button where possible. Feeding has three (Left, Right, Finish). Apple allows pause and resume controls; it stays, but the expanded and Lock Screen versions should match.
!StandBy shows the Lock Screen card twice the size, tinted red at night. Worth a layout of its own (see Puggle, below).

Puggle in the Live Activity

Apple allows a logo mark "without a container", never the whole app icon. Puggle's face, drawn in one colour, can be that mark, and her face can say something: awake, asleep, feeding.

A rough stand-in for her mark: awake, asleep, feeding. The real one would come from her drawing.

P1 · No Puggle. Initials on each child's colour everywhere, as now. Clearest about whose card it is.
