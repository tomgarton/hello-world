What each level can do

  • Parent: everything. Logs, changes and deletes any entry, edits children, sets medicine rules, exports.
  • Helper: logs sleeps, feeds, nappies, medicine and the rest. Can change or delete their own entries. Can't touch anyone else's entries, children, medicine rules or the family's data.
  • Viewer: sees Today, History and Insights. Every log button is hidden.

What sits behind this

  • Helper is the app's own rule. iCloud only knows "can change" and "can't change". A helper's phone is allowed to change things, and the app hides Edit and Delete. That stops accidents. It would not stop someone determined.
  • Viewer is enforced by iCloud itself. A viewer's phone can't change anything, whatever the app shows.
  • One phone invites and removes. Only the person whose iCloud holds the family can do it, so a second parent can't either. The word "owner" never appears. Other phones get a line saying who to ask.
  • Each person's level is saved with the family. That adds one small record per person to what syncs, so it needs a yes before it is built.
  • Names come from iCloud once someone joins, and they can change the name shown beside what they log.
  • Choosing days is left out for now.
  • None of this can be fully tested with one iPhone. Inviting, joining, changing a level and leaving all need a second iPhone on a different Apple Account.
