Repeat patterns

Seven repeats made from the brand's eight soft shapes. Each is tone on tone: a soft field, with shapes a little darker or lighter. For each: at full size, at half size, and in all six colours.

Toss

A tossed repeat: pebbles and quatrefoils in three sizes, turned every way, so the grid is hard to find.

Tile 132 by 132, 8 shapes.

Pills

Pills and half pills leaning left and right, with small dots between. A brick repeat.

Tile 112 by 96, 7 shapes.

Bloom

Blossoms in a half-drop, each with a pale middle, and small quatrefoils between them.

Tile 104 by 104, 10 shapes.

Comb

Soft hexagons packed like honeycomb, with a lighter one here and there and one darker.

Tile 120 by 102, 12 shapes.

Sparkle

Quatrefoils as sparkles, large and small. The brand's stars.

Tile 148 by 148, 9 shapes.

Scales

Scallops in overlapping rows, like roof tiles or waves.

Tile 43.2 by 28, 2 shapes.

Check

Soft squares in a check, two tones, with a small quatrefoil cut into every other one.

Tile 84 by 84, 6 shapes.
