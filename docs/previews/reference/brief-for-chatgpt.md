# Puggle: handover brief

Updated 14 November 2026, after checking everything against the brand book.

Send three things together:

1. **The brand book** (`Puggle-brand-2026-11-14.pdf`, 40 pages). The base for the brand.
2. **The twelve reference pages.** These show what the app draws today, which is ahead of the brand book in places.
3. **App screens and flows** (`Puggle-app-screens-and-flows.pdf`): who it is for, the personas, every flow, and the app's screens.
4. **This brief.** It says what is fixed, where things have moved on, and what is open.

**Where this brief and the brand book differ, this brief is newer.** The brand book was made before the animation research of 14 November. Three things changed after it: she is drawn from the front only, she has a lick of hair, and hair, ears and spines are joined to the head as one shape.

## What Puggle is

Puggle is the character in an iPhone baby and family tracker. She is a platypus, a girl, two and a half: a big sister who is potty training, moving to a big bed and having big feelings. She stands in a small scene at the top of the app and reacts quietly when a parent logs a feed, a sleep or a nappy. Parents use it tired, often at night, so she is calm and never in the way.

## Fixed rules

**Her face**
- No mouth. Her bill never opens, bends, smiles or changes.
- Dot eyes, low on the head and wide apart. Soft oval cheeks.
- Eyebrows appear only with a feeling: raised for delight, soft for content and sleepy. Never at rest, and never angry, sad or worried.
- Feeling shows through her eyes, eyebrows, cheeks, small floating marks, her body and her tail.

**Her tail shows the feeling**, one position each:
- Calm: resting, slightly raised
- Happy: up and wagging
- Proud: straight up, like a flag
- Surprised: stiff and straight out
- Shy: tucked in close
- Sleepy: curled round her feet
- Sad: drooping. Only for her own stories, never about what a parent did or did not log.

**Her body**
- About two and a half heads tall: head 1, body three-quarters, legs a third.
- Her head never squashes or stretches. Her body can, a little.
- Hands are always visible and clear of her head and body in outline. Raised arms go up and out at about 45 degrees. Hands held in front sit on her dress, never over her face or bill. "Oh!" is hands to her cheeks. Peekaboo is the only time hands cover her face.
- Bare feet, webbed.

**Her look**
- A blue A-line dress with a light blue pocket, and a red four-petal flower clip, top right.
- Her fur, bill, dress and clip are never recoloured. No black line.
- **A lick of hair (newer than the brand book, which says "No hair").** One small leaning lick, left of centre, clear of her clip. It is hidden under a hat.
- **One head, one outline (applies to the whole cast).** Whatever grows from a head is part of it: the same fur, the same line, joined with no line between, so it changes the head's outline. Hair, ears and spines all follow this. Kip's, Digby's and Sunny's ears run into their heads. Quill's spines are the brown of her head, not cream. Nan's bun is joined to her head. Pebble's curl is round and central; Puggle's lick is pointed and leans, so the two are not confused.

**Drawing**
- Flat colour. No texture, shading, gradients or highlights in the eyes.
- Each outline is a darker shade of the colour it goes round.
- The world has no outlines. Only characters have a line.
- Sparkles are four-petal shapes, never plain stars.
- Objects have no faces. Faces belong to characters.
- Under about 100 points tall: no nostrils or pocket, and a line a quarter heavier.

**Views**
- **Front only (newer than the brand book).** She always faces the viewer. No three-quarter or side views, and no smooth head turns. To look at someone she slides her eyes and leans her head a little.
- The brand book's model sheet shows three-quarter, side and back views, and its motion board calls three-quarter "her acting view". Those boards predate this decision. Their poses (potty dance, flop-sit, tippy-toe reach) are still wanted, redrawn from the front.
- A back view may still be useful in a story, such as her looking out of a window.

**How she moves**
- Reactions are snappy: hold each pose and pop to the next. Breathing, sleeping and scenes stay smooth.
- One thing at a time. When she moves, the scene behind her goes still.
- Nothing waits for her. Saving and navigating are instant; she reacts alongside.
- Night, or a child asleep: everything half the size and slower, and no jumps.
- No guilt. She is never sad or disappointed about what was logged.
- Silent by default.
- With Reduce Motion on: still poses that swap, no movement.

**Her reactions**

**Places and culture**
- Nothing Indigenous or with strong Indigenous meaning. Generic places only. No Sydney Opera House.
- Original shapes and colours of our own. References are for study; nothing is copied.
- A child's real name never appears in the work.

## Where the app has moved ahead of the brand book

The reference pages show these; the brand book does not yet.

- **The cast is redrawn** with Puggle's 12 November changes: plain oval cheeks (no stitch marks), her eye size, her arms, filled pockets.
- **Grown-ups are shorter** than on the canvas line-up: about a quarter taller than Puggle, where the canvas has them much taller.
- **Noses** are nearer the real animals: a tall koala nose, a broad wombat pad, a small quokka nose on a pale muzzle, a slimmer echidna snout.
- **Feet:** each friend has soft toed feet with small rounded nails. No claws.
- **Clothes:** each character's garment comes in several brand colours. In the cold everyone has a scarf and mittens in their own colour; in the wet a yellow raincoat and rain hat, or an umbrella for those who cannot wear a hat; in the heat a sun hat, a parasol or zinc.
- **Scenes:** four outdoor scenes (hills, beach, backyard, bush), each with a small animal friend, and four rooms (tea table, living room, play room, bedroom).
- **Evenings:** from 18:00 she is indoors, in pyjamas at bedtime. She is outdoors after dark only with a grown-up.
- **Company:** on some days a family member or friend stands with her.

## Open: ideas welcome

- **Arms that bend at the elbow**, so she can hold a book, hug her rabbit, or put a hand under her chin (reference page 3).
- **Mum and Dad** are too alike in outline, and too like a tall Puggle. Ways to tell them apart by shape.
- **Things two characters do together:** sitting side by side, holding hands, passing a ball. One moves at a time.
- **More outfits for Puggle** without recolouring her: a small print on her dress (quatrefoil, gum leaf or wattle) is the proposal.
- **A flat version** with no outline, for prints and small sizes.
- **How she walks from the front**, now that a side-on walk is out.
- **Proposed movement** on reference pages 6 and 7: a blink that shuts fast and opens slowly, eyes that lead a head tilt, a hop with more weight.
- **The logo**, Pebble's name, and whether the app icon keeps a dark line.
