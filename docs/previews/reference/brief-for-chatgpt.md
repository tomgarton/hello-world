# Puggle: reference brief

Paste this with the twelve reference pages. The pages show how the characters look and move today; this says what is fixed and what is open.

## What Puggle is

Puggle is the character in an iPhone baby and family tracker. She is a platypus, a girl, two and a half years old: curious, kind, playful, a little shy. She stands in a small scene at the top of the app and reacts quietly when a parent logs a feed, a sleep or a nappy. The app is used by tired parents, often at night, so she is calm.

## Fixed rules: please keep these

- **No mouth.** Her bill never opens, smiles or changes. Feeling comes from her eyes, eyebrows, cheeks, the tilt of her head and her body.
- **Always facing the viewer.** No side or three-quarter views, and no smooth head turns.
- **Flat colour, one outline.** No fur texture, shading, gradients or highlights in the eyes. Each outline is a darker shade of the colour it goes round.
- **Dot eyes**, low on the head and wide apart. Soft oval cheeks.
- **Her head never squashes or stretches.** Her body can, a little.
- **Eyebrows only with a feeling**, and never angry, sad or worried.
- **Her look:** a blue A-line dress with a light blue pocket, and a red four-petal flower clip, top right. One accessory only.
- **The world has no outlines.** Only characters have a line. Backgrounds are flat shapes.
- **Bare feet.** Webbed for the platypus family; the friends have soft toed feet with small rounded nails. No claws.

## Where she is

- **Outdoors by day:** hills, beach, backyard, bush. Each has a small animal friend (a bird, a crab, a snail, a kookaburra).
- **Indoors from 18:00:** the tea table, the living room or play room, then her bedroom in pyjamas.
- **Out after dark** only with a grown-up.
- Clothes follow the weather: raincoat and rain hat or an umbrella, scarf and mittens, sun hat.

## How she moves

Small, quiet moves with pauses between: breathing, blinking, a head tilt, a nod, a wave, a hop for a milestone, a stretch on waking. Proposed refinements are on pages 6 and 7.

## What is open

- A tuft of hair on top of her head (page 3).
- Arms that bend at the elbow, so she can hold a book, hug her rabbit or touch her face (page 3).
- Mum and Dad are too alike in outline, and too like a tall Puggle. Ideas for telling them apart by shape are welcome.
- Things two characters do together: sitting side by side, holding hands, passing a ball.
- More poses and small moments for a calm toddler, within the fixed rules above.
