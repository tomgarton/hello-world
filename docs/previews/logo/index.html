How to stay on the line

  • One playful thing at a time. Puggle is already round, bright and cute. If the lettering is too, the whole thing tips into a toy. Let her be the play and the type be the grown-up.
  • Spend less. Fewer colours, more empty sand, a small mark. Restraint is most of what reads as expensive.
  • Soft shapes, serious spacing. Rounded letters stay friendly, and a small line in spaced capitals beside them adds the polish.
  • Dusty, not primary. Her colours taken down a step (clay, sage, deep blue, cream) keep the warmth and lose the crayon box.
  • Write to the parent. The app is for the adult at three in the morning. The child gets the stamps and the play pad.
