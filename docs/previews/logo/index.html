Where I'd look first

  • 1 or 2 as the main lockup. Her face is the most recognisable thing you have, and it is already the app icon.
  • 3 or 13 for tiny sizes, where a face blurs: the flower alone still reads as her.
  • 8 if you want the logo to be about the family and not the animal. It also gives the building screen a reason to exist: the shapes that fall become the mark.
  • 14 is the one with an idea in it. It is the only lockup that explains the name.

These are sketches in code, not finished artwork. Spacing, the exact face and the colours would all be tuned on whichever you pick.

PART 2

Building your family

The shapes drop in with gravity, bump and settle, then each finds its place and takes a name. It plays after the last setup question, in place of a plain "You're set".

Building your family
Ollie, Bea and everyone who looks after them.
Open Today

How it would work

  • One shape for each person, in their colour, with their initial. Grown-ups are larger, children smaller. A few plain shapes come along for the ride.
  • About three seconds, and a tap anywhere finishes it at once. It is a small celebration, not a loading bar: nothing is being waited for, so it never shows a percentage or pretends to work.
  • Then the names fade in and the button wakes up. Puggle could cheer beside it.
  • Reduce Motion shows the finished family straight away.
  • The same shapes could then be each person's picture until they add a photo, and each child's, which would also fix twins who share an initial and differ only by colour.
  • In the app this is a small physics scene. The sketch here uses simple round collisions, so shapes overlap a little at their corners.
