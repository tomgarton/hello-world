Breastfeeding, a parent and baby

The International Breastfeeding Symbol, by Matt Daigle, 2006. Its maker gave it to the public domain, so it can be used and redrawn. Below, after the current icon, are three of a parent holding a wrapped baby and then the symbol redrawn six ways, all for this set: the set’s line and weight, round ends, and one spot of colour. Each is shown in light and dark, in the small sizes, in one colour for lists, and on Today tiles beside the bottle, nappy, sleep and solids icons.
