Puggle icons, three sizes

Small is for lists: the drawing as it was. Regular is for tiles: the same shapes with a finer line and a little more detail, shown at 40 points. Large, for where an icon stands alone, is not drawn yet. Under each pair: the small drawing in a list at 20 and 17 points, then the regular one on a tile and in dark.
