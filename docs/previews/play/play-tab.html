A tab for play

Drawn, not built. Stamping is the thing a child asks for, and it is three taps deep in a parent's app. A tab would give it, the stamp book and the stickers one home, with room for Puggle and friends' books later.

As built today Built

Four tabs. Play is a row on Today that opens the pad. The stamp book is on each child’s page under Family, and stickers are beside it.

For it
  • One tap from anywhere to the thing a child asks for.
  • The stamp book and stickers stop being hidden under Family.
  • Books have somewhere to go without another rearrangement later.
Against it
  • Five tabs in an app for parents, one of them for a child.
  • With only the pad and one stamp book it is a thin page.
  • A family with a young baby has no use for it yet.
For you to decide
  • Add the tab now, or when the first book is ready.
  • Whether it shows for every family, or only once a child is old enough to play (about 18 months) or is potty training.
  • Its name: Play, or Puggle.
