Play: things to hand a child

The Play row on Today and its shelf are built, with Draw and the Stamp book. This page refines the two things that would come next, Shapes and Look, and says how each behaves. Both are sketches.

SHAPES

The heap, as a toy

The same shapes that finish first run, without the title or the button. Drag a shape to throw it, or use the slider to tip the phone.

✕
TiltThe phone's own tilt moves the heap. It needs no permission.
TouchEvery finger is followed, so two children can push at once. This sketch follows one.
OutlinesThe app uses each shape's true outline, so they sit flush. Here they collide as circles and leave small gaps.
SoundSilent. A soft knock when shapes land is possible, and would be off until a parent turns it on.
LeavingHold the close button for a moment, as on the play pad.
A call, or leaving the appThe shapes stop. Coming back shows the same screen, still behind the cover.
Reduce MotionThe shapes move only when dragged. Tilt is off.
NightThe darker versions of the same colours.
LOOK

Black, white and red, for a young baby

Bold black, white and red patterns are what books and cards made for young babies use. Here six patterned shapes drift slowly on a dark screen.

MovementShapes glide and turn. Nothing blinks or changes suddenly.
What the app says"Something to look at." It makes no claim about development, in keeping with the rule on medical advice.
When it showsOffered on the shelf while a child is under six months. A parent can show or hide it at any age.
EndingA parent picks 2, 5 or 10 minutes. The shapes then slow to a stop and stay as a still picture until a parent holds the close button. Nothing is counted.
Brightness and soundBrightness is left as the phone has it. Silent.
TouchA touch does nothing, so a baby's hand can't change it.
Reduce MotionOne still pattern, changed by a parent's tap.

One thing to settle. flows.md says the night look has no white anywhere. Look is white on black by design. After dark it could swap white for the warm cream, or stay as it is on the grounds that a parent chose to open it.

The last four are ideas only. I'd build Shapes and Look, watch what your own children go back to, and add from there.

Everything on the shelf works with no signal. None of it is counted, shows in Insights or sends a reminder. The time limit applies to Shapes and Look; Draw and the Stamp book run until a parent closes them.

Whether one app should hold both a parent's tracker and a child's play is still your call. Keeping play behind one row is what lets it be both. It could be paid for later.

The shelf

As built, with the two new cards outlined. The Stamp book opens as a sheet; the other three open behind the cover.

A press on the app icon and a Control Centre button could both open the shelf straight from a locked phone. Neither is built. A Play section in Settings would hold the time limit, the sound, and which cards show.

Shapes as people's pictures was in the last round. It is left out here because flows.md gives each child a character or a photo in a round frame, and the two ideas need choosing between.
