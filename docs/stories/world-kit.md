# The world kit: what the creek is made of

16 November 2026, first list. Tom: the things that fill the scenes need writing down: items, objects,
animals, accessories, plants and buildings. This is the list for the creek and its six places, so that a
picture in one book matches the same place in the next.

**How to read it.** "Have" means it is drawn already, in the app's scenes or on the canvas's props boards.
"Rough" means the storyboard has a stand-in shape for it (`Thing` in `BoardBook.swift`). "To draw" means
nothing exists. Where "Have" is from the spec and not checked against the drawing, it says "check".

**Rules for everything here** (from the brand book): the world is flat shapes with no outline; only the
cast has a line. Palette colours only. One white highlight at most. No brands, logos or words on anything.
Plants and animals are real ones from eastern Australia, drawn simply and named plainly.

## The six places

| Place | The building or home | Always there | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The burrow** | A round door in the creek bank, a window beside it, a chimney pipe in the grass above | The still pool, a washing line, the basket by the door, a row of boots | Door: rough. Rest: to draw |
| **The log pile** | Stacked logs with gaps for doors and windows | Quill's rows of cones and stones, a workbench stump | Logs: rough. Rest: to draw |
| **The big gum** | A tall tree with a platform, a rope ladder and a hollow | A swing, a lookout, bark in long strips | Trunk and leaves: rough. Rest: to draw |
| **The paddock** | Mounds with round doors, behind a post-and-rail fence | A wheelbarrow, a spade, a vegetable patch | Mound: rough. Rest: to draw |
| **The sandy bend** | A low house on stilts over the sand, with a deck | Towels, buckets, a striped umbrella, a rope swing over the water | Towel, bucket: rough. Rest: to draw |
| **Nan and Pop's** | A small house with a porch across the stepping stones | The kettle, the biscuit tin, Pop's hat, Nan's pots, a rocking chair | House, tin, hat, stones: rough. Rest: to draw |

Also: **the bridge** by the big gum, **the creek** itself (still pool, shallows, the wide bend), and
**the map** that opens every book. All to draw.

Rooms the app has already, which are the inside of the burrow: dining, living, playroom, bedroom (Have).
Outdoor scenes the app has: hills, beach, backyard, bush (Have). None of these is the creek yet.
