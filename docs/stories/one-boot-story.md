# One Boot

*A lift-the-flap story. For ages 2 to 4, read together.*

---

**1**

It is a puddle day.
Puggle has her boots.

**One boot on. One boot gone.**

---

**2**

Is it under the logs?

*(lift the log)*

No boot. A cone! And Quill.
"Not yet," says Quill. Then she comes too.

**One boot on. One boot gone.**

---

**3**

Is it down the hole?

*(lift the dirt)*

No boot. A flower! And Digby.
"Me too!" says Digby.

**One boot on. One boot gone.**

---

**4**

Is it up the tree?

*(lift the leaves)*

No boot. A leaf! And Kip.
"I can see the whole creek," says Kip. "Wait for me."

---

**5**

Is it under the towel?

*(lift the towel)*

No boot. A bucket! And Sunny.
"The rule is," says Sunny, "we look together."

**One boot on. One boot gone.**

---

**6**

Is it under Pop's hat?

*(lift the hat)*

No boot. A duck!
"Well, I never," says Nan.

---

**7**

Is it in the tin?

*(lift the lid)*

No boot. A biscuit.
Two, at Nan's.

---

**8**

Step, squelch. Step, squelch.

**One boot on. One boot gone.**

---

**9**

Is it in the basket?

*(lift the blanket)*

Ba!

---

**10**

Puggle looks at the boot.
Pebble looks at Puggle.
"Mine," says Puggle. Then: "Ba."

---

**11**

No boots on.
Splash.

And the creek went on by.
