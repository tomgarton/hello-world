# How Puggle writes

Drawn from Apple's Human Interface Guidelines (Foundations → Writing) and Monzo's published writing principles, adapted for a tired parent holding a baby with one hand.

## The voice

Plain, kind and calm. A friend who knows the app, not a nurse and not a coach. British and Australian spelling. No em dashes.

- **Clear first.** Say what it does, in as few words as it takes. Read it out loud: if you wouldn't say it to a friend, rewrite it.
- **What matters to the reader comes first.** The effect before the reason. "Saved. Undo" not "Your entry has been saved successfully".
- **Normal words, not formal ones.** "Can't", "it's", "you'll". Start a sentence with "But" or "So" if it reads better.
- **Active voice.** Say who does what. "Siri starts the timer", not "The timer is started".
- **No jargon.** If a technical word is needed (CSV, Dynamic Island), say what it is the first time.

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Tone |
|---|---|
| Logging, settings, helper text | Plain and short. No jokes. |
| Medicines, health, anything worrying | Plainest of all. Never advice, never a judgement. |
| Errors | What happened, then what to do. No "oops", no blame. |
| Firsts, finished cards, stickers | A little warmth is welcome. One light touch, never a pun. |
| Night | Fewer words. |

Delight belongs in the pictures and movement (Puggle, the scenes, the stamps), not in the words. Humour, if any, is rare and never at anyone's expense.

## Patterns

- **Buttons start with a verb**: "Save bottle", "Add a photo", "Start sleep". Not "OK" or "Let's go".
- **Sentence case everywhere**: titles, buttons, headers. Pick it once and keep it.
- **Settings**: label what it does when on; people infer the opposite. One line of help, at most two.
- **Point, don't describe**: link straight to a setting instead of describing where it is. When the setting lives in iOS (Action button, Focus), number the steps.
- **Empty screens** say what will appear and how to make it happen.
- **Possessives sparingly**: "Reminders", not "Your reminders", unless it is about the person (You, Your data).
- **Never "we"** in the app. Say what the app does: "The app never suggests a dose."
- **Tap**, never "click".
- **Numbers**: digits, units spaced ("150 ml"), 24-hour or the phone's setting for times.
- **Interpuncts** join short facts: "Bottle · 150 ml · formula".

## Words to use and avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| child, little one | baby (once they're walking), kiddo |
| sleep, nap | slumber, snooze |
| feed, bottle, breastfeed | nursing (unless the parent chooses it) |
| due | overdue, late, missed |
| Puggle (splash, icon and Help only) | the app's name in everyday screens |
| | streak, goal, target, should, must, don't forget |
