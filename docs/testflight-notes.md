## First build for testers outside the family (draft, 23 November 2026)

Three pieces for App Store Connect. The first two go under TestFlight, Test Information. The third is the
build's "What to Test".

### Beta App Description

Puggle is a baby tracker for the whole family. Log feeds, sleeps, nappies, the potty, meals and medicines for
every child on one screen, with one hand. Share the day with the people who help, through your own iCloud.
There's no account to make, and no adverts.

### Beta App Review notes

No sign-in is needed. To see the app with entries in it, tap "Try out a sample family" on the first screen.
The sample family is made up, and leaving it removes it.

Entries are kept on the phone and in the person's own iCloud, which they can share with people they invite.
The app has no server.

### What to Test

Thank you for trying Puggle. It keeps track of the day for every child in your family.

Please try:

- Setting up your family, or tap "Try out a sample family" to look around first.
- Logging a feed, a nappy and a sleep from Today.
- Starting a sleep, then locking your phone. The time shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
- Ending the sleep from the Lock Screen with "Woke up".
- Adding a medicine with a gap between doses. The next dose shows on the Lock Screen.
- Looking after: baths, teeth, nails, haircuts and visits.
- History and Insights after a few days.
- The Apple Watch app, if you have one.
- Inviting someone else in the family, if they're testing too.

Good to know:

- Anyone you invite needs to be testing Puggle as well.
- Puggle gives no medical advice and never suggests a dose.
- Tap Puggle and the people around her. They answer.

To send feedback, take a screenshot while Puggle is open, then tap Share Beta Feedback. Anything that looks
wrong, feels slow or is hard to find is useful.

## 0.1.0, first build (November 2026)

Puggle keeps track of feeds, sleep, nappies and the potty for every child in the family.

Please try:

- Going through the first pages and adding a child.
- Logging a feed, a nappy and a sleep from Today.
- Starting a sleep, closing the app, and stopping it later.
- Settings, Appearance and night, Icons: change the icon colour.
- The app icon on your Home Screen in dark, clear and tinted.

Good to know:

- Entries sync through iCloud. Sharing with another person needs both phones on this build.
- "Sample family" in Settings shows made-up entries. Yours are safe.

Tell Tom about anything that looks wrong, feels slow or is hard to find.
