# Tips and links: for a GP to review

Puggle is a baby tracker. Parents log feeds, sleeps and nappies. This document lists every place the app would point a parent to health information, and the rule that decides when. The links and the bedtime rule are built, in Insights under Patterns. The wet-nappy rule is not.

## What the app does and doesn't do

- It **links** to pages on the NHS website. It never rewords, summarises or quotes them, so all the advice is the NHS's own.
- Each link has a **short line written by us** above it, such as "Around 6 months". Those lines, and the **rules for when each link appears**, are what we'd like checked.
- It **never** says what is wrong with a child, what is normal for them, or what a parent should do.
- Every link carries the credit "Information from the NHS website", as the NHS website terms ask.

## Shown by age

| When | Our line | Links to |
|---|---|---|
| Under 12 months, at the foot of Insights, Patterns | "About sleep at this age" | [NHS: Helping your baby to sleep](https://www.nhs.uk/baby/caring-for-a-newborn/helping-your-baby-to-sleep/) |
| From 5 months 2 weeks until 9 months, in the same place | "Around 6 months, solid foods come in" | [NHS Start for Life: Weaning](https://www.nhs.uk/start-for-life/baby/weaning/) |
| From 12 months, in the same place | "About sleep at this age" | [NHS: Sleep and young children](https://www.nhs.uk/baby/health/sleep-and-young-children/) |

Questions:

1. Are these the right pages?
2. Is "from 5 months 2 weeks" a sensible time to mention weaning, given the NHS says around 6 months?
3. For a baby born early, should the app hold back age-based links, or leave them? It doesn't know a due date today.

## Shown from the child's own data

These describe what was logged, then offer a page. They never say why.

| Rule | Our line | Links to |
|---|---|---|
| The middle bedtime over the last 14 days is 45 minutes or more later than over the 14 days before, with at least 7 bedtimes logged in each | "Bedtimes in the last two weeks were about 1h 05m later than in the two weeks before." (the figure is the real difference) | The sleep page for the child's age, as above |

**Proposed, and not to be built without your say:**

| Rule | Our line | Links to |
|---|---|---|
| A child under 12 months has had half or fewer of their usual wet nappies in the last 24 hours, where "usual" is the previous 7 days and at least 3 days were logged | "Fewer wet nappies were logged in the last 24 hours than usual." | [NHS: Dehydration, symptoms](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dehydration/symptoms) |

Questions:

4. Should the wet-nappy prompt exist at all? A missed log looks the same as a missed nappy, so it will sometimes fire wrongly.
5. If it should, is the threshold right, and should it say more, such as "If you're worried, call 111"?
6. Is there anything here that you think moves the app towards giving medical advice?

## Not planned

- Advice written by the app itself, by AI or otherwise.
- Normal ranges, targets, or comparisons with other people's children.
- Anything about medicine doses. The app only checks a dose against limits the parent typed from the box.
