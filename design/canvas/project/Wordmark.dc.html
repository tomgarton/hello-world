Wordmark

Chosen 15 November 2026
Six letters drawn for the name, lower case, in one round weight that matches her shapes. The two g counters sit like her dot eyes, and the bar of the e keeps a slight slant. It is a drawing, not a typeface: do not set other words in it. Bricolage Grotesque stays the display type.
How it is built
Stems are 35 to 37 wide on a 688 wide drawing. Gaps are 7 between a straight side and a round one, and 11 between the g and the l. The colour is Ink, #3A2A20, or Paper on a dark ground.
