Set pieces and backgrounds

Places from a Sydney family's week, in the house style. Second pass: one set of scene rules, so every place feels like the same world.
Scene rules
Two horizons. Open places (beach, bush, harbour) at 40%; enclosed places (backyard, park, rain, pool, Grandma’s, street) at 70%. The set reads as one world.
A stage. The lower middle stays clear for the cast. Props go to the sides and the back.
One scale. Puggle is 70 units tall in a 390 × 300 scene. Props are sized to her: gumboots to her shin, a bucket to her knee, the umbrella about twice her height.
Line weight shows distance. Far things get a thin, softened line; the middle 1.8; the front 2.2. Big flat areas (sea, sand, lawn) have no outline, only a line at the horizon.
Light and weather per scene. The sun appears only where it helps; three cloud shapes; one hero object and two or three supporting ones.
Beach
Morning. Low horizon, one wave row, the pine fully in frame, flags planted behind; umbrella and towel left, castle and bucket right.
Bush walk
Afternoon haze. Far ridges in a soft line; a foreground gum frames the left, a log and ferns hold the right corner; the track runs to the lookout.
Sydney Harbour
Golden hour, the sun setting inside the arch. Sandstone towers on the arch, a calmer skyline, the ferry with its wake; the cast stands at the railing.
Backyard
Hills Hoist with real washing (tee, onesie, towel), a water-fan sprinkler, the lemon tree and a paddling pool with depth.
The park
Swings and slide share the back ground line; the gum sits in frame on the right; the rug comes forward on the left.
Rainy day
Overcast. Rain falls in an even slant, puddles ripple, toddler-sized gumboots by the big puddle, a snail, a rainbow far off.
The pool
Flat-on: back hedge, a tensioned shade sail with stitched edge and its shadow, lane ropes across the water, light tiles, bunting along the top, the ring in the pool.
Grandma's house
The cottage moves left; the garden is the stage. The path comes in from the side; a low fence lets the hydrangeas show; puffs of chimney smoke.
The café
A corner café: striped awning, big window, a table with a babyccino, the A-frame board and potted ferns.
The farm
Open country: a red shed, the water tank and a windmill, a wire fence, sheep and a hay bale.
The shops
The local strip: grocer, bakery and florist with picture signs, fruit crates and buckets of flowers on the footpath.
Night street
Terraces set back with mixed roofs, the lamp between houses, a low wall with the cat, one lit doorway.
