The Puggle style

Taken from everything so far: the calm line of system icon sets, the duotone accent from Phosphor, the two-tone fills and sticker energy of the nursery sets, and our own clothing label. What makes it Puggle: chubby, soft shapes, colour by activity from the brand palette, the quatrefoil sparkle, and finishes that carry from the app to stickers and clothing.

Six rules
1. Chubby
Squat, toddler proportions. Wide bodies, short necks, nothing spindly; most icons fill the 20 pt keyshape.
2. Soft
1.4 pt line, round caps and joins, every corner rounded. No sharp points, even on stars.
3. Colour by activity
One brand colour fills one main part, inside the line. Feeding and milestones Wattle, sleep Sky, nappy, potty and bath Mint, play and outings Blush, health Shell. Scans fast on Today.
4. Quatrefoil sparkle
Sparkles and highlights use the four-petal shape from Puggle’s hair clip, never a plain star.
5. No faces in the app
Objects stay objects. Faces and expressions belong to the characters, in stickers, books and print.
6. One drawing, three finishes
Line for the app, sticker for celebrations and widgets, print for clothing and wallpapers. The slipped, off-register line lives only in print.
