Rules
  • 1.75 pt stroke at 24 pt, round caps and joins, to sit beside SF Symbols Regular
  • One accent fill, under the line, up to about a third of the shape. Wattle by default
  • Must read with the accent removed (mono test below)
  • No faces on icons; faces belong to the characters
  • Shipped as custom SF Symbols so they scale with Dynamic Type and take weights
