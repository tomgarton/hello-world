Drawing Puggle by hand

1. Head
2. Eyes and bill
3. Cheeks and body
4. Arms, legs, tail
  • Start from two ovals: the head, and the bill overlapping its lower edge.
  • Eyes sit on the head’s centre line, close above the bill, 54 apart.
  • Cheeks sit at the ends of the bill’s top edge, half on the head.
  • The jumper is the body. Arms join just under the head; legs are short and thick.
  • Outline last, in one weight per part; round every corner.
