Set pieces and backgrounds

Places from a Sydney family's week, redrawn as pictures: flat shapes, a small colour key, real light, and the cast in the middle of something.
1. No outlines on the world
Places are flat shapes. Only the cast has a line, a darker shade of each colour, so they are always the first thing you see.
2. A colour key
Three or four colours per scene, shown as dots beside each name. Light and shade are tones of those colours.
3. One big shape
Each scene has one shape that owns it: an umbrella, an arch, a roof, a tree. Things overlap and crop at the edges.
4. Real light
Every scene knows where the sun is: a lit side, a shade side and a cast shadow on the ground.
5. A moment
The cast is doing something, in their small versions, about 86 units tall in a 390 × 300 scene.
Beach
A giant striped umbrella and its shadow. Puggle shows off her sandcastle; Pebble stays in the shade.
Bush walk
A big gum frames the right and throws its shadow across the track. Puggle and Quill set off.
Sydney Harbour
The sun sets inside the arch and lays a path on the water. Puggle waves at the ferry with Pop.
Backyard
Morning. The sun over the fence, a magpie singing, Puggle stretching and Pebble already up.
The park
A huge gum with dappled shade. Puggle and Kip in front of the slide and swings.
Rainy day
Heavy cloud, even rain, one big puddle. Puggle jumps in it in her yellow raincoat; Digby keeps his distance.
The pool
Sunlit water with lane ropes and a ring, a shade sail and bunting. Puggle and Sunny on the deck.
Grandma's house
The red roof fills the corner; Nan waits on the verandah as Puggle runs up the path.
The café
One long awning and its shadow. Mum and Puggle, and a babyccino on the table.
The farm
A windmill towers over wheat and paddock. Puggle and Digby meet the sheep.
The shops
Grocer, bakery, florist, told apart by their picture signs. Fruit crates crop the corner; Puggle and Quill do the shopping.
Night street
Bedtime. The moon behind the terraces, each house keeping a whisper of its daytime colour.
