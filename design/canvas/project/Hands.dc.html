Hands

A. Fur-brown hands, slate feet
Hands match the body, so they read as paws. Feet stay slate, like webbed platypus feet, tying them to the bill.
B. All fur-brown
Hands and feet both match the body. The calmest; the bill becomes the only slate shape.
C. Light tan hands and feet
A lighter warm tan, like paw pads. Hands stand out against both the fur and the sleeves.
