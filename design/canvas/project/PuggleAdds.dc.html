Puggle: three additions

Proposals only. Her face and proportions are unchanged; these add range, they don't redesign her.
1. A patterned outfit
Her dress takes a small print from our motif family. Sleeves stay plain so the arms still read. Each print is a real fabric repeat.

2. A flat version
No outline at all: shapes and tones only. Arms and legs are a shade deeper than the dress and fur so they separate. For prints, wallpapers and small sizes.
On colour
Sits on Wattle without a line.

Small sizes
Holds down to icon size, where the outlined version clogs.
3. Walking
A side-on walk in two key poses: the stride, and the passing step with a small bounce. The tail and arms swing against the legs. Enough for books now, and the two keys Rive needs later.
Stride
Legs apart, arms opposite.
Passing
Legs together, body lifts.
In a row
Alternating the two poses makes a parade.
