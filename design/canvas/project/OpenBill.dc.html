Open bill: superseded

Decision: the bill never changes. Puggle emotes through her eyes, floating marks and body language.
A. Hinged bill
Upper and lower bill part, with a dark gap and a tongue when laughing. Most like a real bill.
B. Shape-changing bill
The bill itself changes shape: a round "O" for surprise, a wide grin for laughing. Like Pingu’s stretchy beak.
C. Inside the outline
The bill keeps its shape; a dark opening appears inside it. Simplest, but close to the drawn mouth you disliked.
D. A plus squash and stretch
The hinged bill, with the whole head stretching tall for surprise and squashing wide for laughter.
B, larger (recommended). Rules: the bill keeps its colour, outline and nostrils, so she always reads as Puggle; only its shape changes. The opening is a dark warm tone, not black. A pink tongue only when laughing. Eyes do the rest: big dots for surprise, happy arcs for laughing.
