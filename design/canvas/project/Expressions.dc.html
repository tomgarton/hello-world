Silhouette test. Arms keep a thin light outline in silhouette so they never merge with the body. Both hands are always visible. Raised or lowered hands sit clear of the head and body; raised arms go up and out at about 45 degrees, never straight up behind the head. Hands held in front sit on the garment, never over the face or bill.
