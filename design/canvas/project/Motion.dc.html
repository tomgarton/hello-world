Motion studies for Rive

Moving sketches of how Puggle and the scenes should feel. They loop here on the canvas; the real animations get rebuilt in the Rive editor from these.
Puggle
Idle
Breathes, blinks every four seconds, tail drifts. The default on Today.
Wave
Hello on launch and on the splash. Arm from the shoulder, head tilts with it.
Hooray
After a log or a milestone. Squash, jump, land; quatrefoils pop.
Sleepy
When a child is asleep or it is night. Slow breath, nodding head.
Walk
Pull to refresh and the books. Two key poses for now; Rive adds the in-betweens.

Blink runs on its own layer so it plays over every state. The app sets the inputs: greet on launch, celebrate after a log or milestone, asleep while a sleep timer runs or at night.
From these studies to Rive
1. Import the parts. Puggle is exported as an SVG with named parts (tail, legs, dress, arm-left, arm-right, head, eyes, bill, clip), saved in the repo. Rive imports it and keeps the names.
2. Rig. Pivots at the shoulders, the neck and the tail root; a bone or two for the tail. No mouth to animate: emotion is eyes, marks and body.
3. Animate and wire. Rebuild these five loops as timelines, then the state machine on the left. One .riv file for the character.
4. Scenes. Each scene is its own artboard with slow ambient loops, with Puggle nested in it. Reduce Motion shows the still scene.
Scenes with ambient motion
Morning
The sun rises and settles, its glow breathes, the washing sways, the magpie bobs, dew twinkles.
Bedtime
Stars twinkle out of step, the moon’s halo breathes, the lamp flickers, a shooting star every nine seconds.
Rainy day
Rain falls on a loop, rings spread on the puddle, Puggle jumps.
Celebration
Confetti falls without a seam, balloons bob, the quatrefoil pulses, Puggle hops.
