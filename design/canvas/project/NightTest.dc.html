Night street, a different approach

A test on one scene before touching the other eleven. Same place, same kit, drawn as a picture, not a set of icons.
Now
Proposed
1. World without outlines
Houses, wall, lamp and branch are flat shapes. Only Puggle keeps the Ink line, so she is the first thing you see.
2. Three colours
Night in four tones, Moon, and Wattle for every light. Her blue pyjamas and red clip are the only other colour.
3. One big shape
The moon is huge and sits behind the roofline. Roofs overlap it; a gum branch crops the top corner.
4. Light
Moonlit right sides and roof edges, glowing windows, a lit doorway, a pool of lamplight and her cast shadow.
5. A moment
Puggle, in pyjamas, out to say goodnight to the moon; the cat watches from the wall.
