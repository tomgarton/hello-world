Activity icons

As built in the app, 15 November 2026
Twenty-two drawings in one hand: a round-ended line in Ink, soft corners, and one flat fill. Objects have no faces. A baby in a drawing has one dot eye and hair, nothing more. These replace “Activity icons, rounded” on the App page.

Small, for lists and menus under 24 points: the fewest shapes and a 2 point line. Regular, for tiles from 32 points: the same shapes, a finer line and a little more detail. Large, for where a drawing stands alone: drawn on a 48 grid. The large drawings are not in the app yet.

The child's colour
On a tile or log for one child. Pumping is the parent's, so it stays Wattle.
Fixed colours never change
Skin, poo, wee and anything with a real colour keep it in every setting.
In dark
The line turns pale and is drawn a fifth thinner, because a pale line on a dark ground looks heavier. Fixed colours are dimmed a little so the line still shows against them.
