What the references taught us
Leave room. The best phone wallpapers are mostly empty: one colour, a small scene or a row of characters at the bottom.
Tone on tone. A pattern in a slightly deeper shade of the background gives richness without noise, and lets the cast stand in front.
One motif family. Ours are the gum leaf, the quatrefoil and the activity icons. They repeat everywhere, like a house pattern.
Line and flat both work. The same drawings as outlines on dark, or as flat shapes on colour.
